Samsung TV (Tizen)

Samsung TVs run Tizen, which cannot run Kodi or Android apps. Kodi add-ons, including Kodaches, do not work there.

What to use instead

  1. A streaming stick (recommended): plug a Google TV / Android TV device or a Fire TV Stick into the Samsung TV and install Kodaches there.
  2. Samsung TV Plus, built into Samsung TVs, carries many of the same free streaming channels.
There is no Samsung installation. Apps outside Samsung's store can only be put on a TV in Developer Mode with a certificate made for that one TV, so a public download cannot be installed.
