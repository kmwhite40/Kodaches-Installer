Samsung TV (Tizen)

Samsung TVs run Tizen, which cannot run Kodi or Android apps. Kodi add-ons, including Kodaches, do not work there.

What to use instead

  1. The Galaxy Samsung app has the same live TV, guide, sports and library, with the same profiles and resume points as Kodaches. Samsung only lets apps outside its store onto a TV in Developer Mode, with a certificate made for that specific TV, so your Galaxy administrator installs it for you. A public download cannot be installed on a Samsung TV.
  2. The TV's web browser: open your Galaxy address (the same one the Galaxy apps use) in the Samsung Internet app. It works with the TV remote; playback depends on the browser.
  3. A streaming stick: plug an Android TV / Google TV device or Fire TV into the Samsung TV and install Kodaches there.
There is no one-click Samsung installation. Samsung Smart TV store publication would be needed for that, and Kodaches is a private household add-on.
