Release notes

2.7.0 · 2026-10-29

  • The Modern look: Arctic Horizon 2 by jurialmunkey, the skin the Diggz Xenon and ArcticFlix builds are modified from, in its original form, with the Kodaches top bar and rows: Live TV (German TV, free channels, favourites), TV Guide, News (World News, US News), Sports (today's games, sports channels), Kids, Free Movies, Streaming Apps, plus your library. Pick it in the build setup (it is preselected) or in Kodaches → Settings & looks → Modern.
  • Your own changes to the Modern menu (made in the skin's menu editor) are kept when Kodaches updates it.
  • A skin or the apps pack whose download fails on a slow Kodi mirror is tried again.
  • Kodaches Repository 1.2.0 lists jurialmunkey's repository (Arctic Horizon 2 and its helpers). Kodi updates the repository by itself. The Modern look needs Kodi 21.

2.6.0 · 2026-10-29

  • One zip, whole build: install Kodaches Build from zip and answer Yes. It adds the Kodaches repository, installs Kodaches and the Kodaches skin from it (so they keep updating themselves), the free apps (Pluto TV, Samsung TV Plus, Plex Live, PBS, YouTube, NewsON, ARD Mediathek, Radio, OpenSubtitles.com, Multi Weather) and sets up the TV guide and streaming settings.
  • Fix: SlyGuy apps (Pluto TV, Samsung TV Plus, Plex Live, Disney+, Paramount+, ESPN, FOX Sports, PBS, Hulu, HBO Max, Roku) did not install unless SlyGuy's repository was already on the device: they need it, and Kodi never installs a repository by itself. Kodaches now installs it first.
  • If a download fails (a slow Kodi mirror), the setup tries again.

2.5.0 · 2026-10-29

  • World News: 20 international news channels (Al Jazeera, BBC News, France 24, CNN International, NHK World, i24NEWS, Africanews, ABC Australia, CBC News, NBC News NOW and others), only from the broadcasters' own servers. On the home menu as News.
  • Faster channel starts: Kodaches gives Kodi one quality of a channel instead of its whole list of qualities, which Kodi would otherwise open one by one before starting (Al Jazeera English: from failing after 30 seconds to 11 seconds).
  • Streaming apps in folders (TV & Movies, News, Sports, German TV, Kids, Music & Radio, Tools), with new official add-ons: Hulu, HBO Max, The Roku Channel, Crackle, Plex, NewsON (local U.S. news), NHK World, CBC News, Tagesschau, ARD and ZDF Mediathek, MediathekView, Arte, ZDFtivi, Radio, SomaFM, Mixcloud, SoundCloud, BBC Sounds, Trakt, Up Next, Backup, AutoCompletion, Library Auto Update, Aerial screensaver, Multi Weather and more.
  • Installing Multi Weather makes it Kodi's weather provider, so the Weather entry on the home menu shows your forecast.
  • Kodaches skin 1.3.0: News and Kids on the home menu, with their own rows.
  • Fix: channels broadcast in several languages (DW English, Spanish, Arabic) are no longer merged into one.

2.3.0 · 2026-10-28

  • Fix: Pluto TV channels are no longer listed. Their server refuses Kodi, so they only showed Playback failed. Watch Pluto TV with the official Pluto TV add-on, now in Streaming apps together with Samsung TV Plus and Plex Live (free, no account, own guide).
  • The background channel check now checks the way Kodi plays, so a channel Kodi cannot play is hidden instead of failing when you pick it.
  • A channel list that fails to download is tried again at once and after 15 minutes, instead of leaving a section such as Local or Sports empty until the next refresh.
  • Kodaches skin 1.2.0: Estuary's own sections are back on the home menu: Movies and TV shows from your library (with movie sets), Music, Pictures, Videos, Kodi Favourites, Weather and the rest, next to the Kodaches entries (now called Free Movies and Favorite Channels).

2.2.0 · 2026-10-28

  • TV guide: Kodaches channels appear in Kodi's own TV section with a programme guide for about 550 channels (Pluto TV, Samsung TV Plus, Plex, Roku, German broadcasters; Pluto TV removed in 2.3.0), through the official IPTV Simple client. Set it up from Settings & looks, or with the build setup.
  • Channel lists show what is on now; TV Guide is on the Kodaches home menu.
  • The guide is rebuilt in the background twice a day, without channels that stopped working.
  • Fix: adaptive streaming (InputStream Adaptive) is off again by default. In testing it crashed Kodi on some German live streams. It is still in Add-on settings → Playback for a channel that buffers a lot.

2.1.0 · 2026-10-28

  • Kodaches build: Settings & looks → Set up the Kodaches build installs the Kodaches skin (based on Kodi's Estuary) with its own home menu and rows, applies streaming settings, and offers the streaming apps.
  • Faster: long channel lists open in pages of 100; the fastest working stream of a channel plays first.
  • Clean-up: a background check finds channels that stopped working and hides them (it pauses while you watch); run it any time from Live TV → Clean up channels.
  • Less buffering: HLS channels play through InputStream Adaptive by default, which lowers quality instead of stalling; the recommended look uses a 64 MB video buffer.
  • Streaming apps: YouTube (with a Karaoke shortcut), Disney+, Paramount+, ESPN, FOX Sports, PBS and OpenSubtitles.com: official add-ons, installed on request, each with your own account where needed.
  • Maintenance: clears downloaded add-on packages and temporary files.

2.0.0 · 2026-10-28

  • Standalone: channels, sports schedules and films come straight from public sources; no server or account needed.
  • Live TV: German, U.S., Texas and Dallas - Fort Worth, sports, news, kids, movies, series, documentary, music and free streaming services; channels in several lists appear once.
  • Backup streams: when a channel's stream fails, Kodaches tries the next one; channels with no working stream are hidden for a day.
  • Sports Center: schedules, live scores and broadcasters for 17 leagues.
  • Movies: public-domain classic films from the Internet Archive.
  • Favorites and recently watched are kept on each device.
  • Removed: the Galaxy server connection (movies and shows from a home server, profiles, recordings).

1.0.2 · 2026-10-28

  • The Galaxy address can be typed as just the IP (for example 192.168.0.10); http:// and port 8080 are added for you.
  • When Galaxy cannot be reached, Kodaches shows the address it tried, the TV's own address and the reason, and lets you try again.

1.0.1 · 2026-10-28

  • Maintenance release: removes an internal server name from a code comment. No changes for viewers.

1.0.0 · 2026-10-28

  • First release of the Kodaches add-on and repository for Kodi 20, 21 and newer.
  • Live TV from Galaxy: German TV, U.S. TV, local stations (Texas, Dallas - Fort Worth), news, kids, movies, music, free streaming services, your own channels; now/next, seven-day guide, guide search, favorites, recently watched.
  • Sports Center: on now, sports channels, league schedules with live scores and the channels that carry each game.
  • Movies and series with resume, Continue Watching, My List, watched status; free public-domain classics.
  • Recordings (DVR) for channels from your IPTV provider.
  • Seven looks using skins from the official Kodi repository; English and German.
  • Automatic updates through Kodi's add-on updater.
