Release notes

1.0.2 · 2026-10-28

  • The Galaxy address can be typed as just the IP (for example 192.168.0.10); http:// and port 8080 are added for you.
  • When Galaxy cannot be reached, Kodaches shows the address it tried, the TV's own address and the reason, and lets you try again.

2.1.0 · 2026-10-28

  • Kodaches build: Settings & looks → Set up the Kodaches build installs the Kodaches skin (based on Kodi's Estuary) with its own home menu and rows, applies streaming settings, and offers the streaming apps.
  • Faster: long channel lists open in pages of 100; the fastest working stream of a channel plays first.
  • Clean-up: a background check finds channels that stopped working and hides them (it pauses while you watch); run it any time from Live TV → Clean up channels.
  • Less buffering: HLS channels play through InputStream Adaptive by default, which lowers quality instead of stalling; the recommended look uses a 64 MB video buffer.
  • Streaming apps: YouTube (with a Karaoke shortcut), Disney+, Paramount+, ESPN, FOX Sports, PBS and OpenSubtitles.com: official add-ons, installed on request, each with your own account where needed.
  • Maintenance: clears downloaded add-on packages and temporary files.

2.0.0 · 2026-10-28

  • Standalone: channels, sports schedules and films come straight from public sources; no server or account needed.
  • Live TV: German, U.S., Texas and Dallas - Fort Worth, sports, news, kids, movies, series, documentary, music and free streaming services; channels in several lists appear once.
  • Backup streams: when a channel's stream fails, Kodaches tries the next one; channels with no working stream are hidden for a day.
  • Sports Center: schedules, live scores and broadcasters for 17 leagues.
  • Movies: public-domain classic films from the Internet Archive.
  • Favorites and recently watched are kept on each device.
  • Removed: the Galaxy server connection (movies and shows from a home server, profiles, recordings).

1.0.1 · 2026-10-28

  • Maintenance release: removes an internal server name from a code comment. No changes for viewers.

1.0.0 · 2026-10-28

  • First release of the Kodaches add-on and repository for Kodi 20, 21 and newer.
  • Live TV from Galaxy: German TV, U.S. TV, local stations (Texas, Dallas - Fort Worth), news, kids, movies, music, free streaming services, your own channels; now/next, seven-day guide, guide search, favorites, recently watched.
  • Sports Center: on now, sports channels, league schedules with live scores and the channels that carry each game.
  • Movies and series with resume, Continue Watching, My List, watched status; free public-domain classics.
  • Recordings (DVR) for channels from your IPTV provider.
  • Seven looks using skins from the official Kodi repository; English and German.
  • Automatic updates through Kodi's add-on updater.
