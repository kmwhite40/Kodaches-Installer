Kodaches for Kodi

Your household's Galaxy server, inside Kodi: free German and U.S. live TV with a guide, local channels, a sports center with schedules, and your own movies and shows, with the same profiles, favorites and resume points as the Galaxy apps.

Kodaches needs a Galaxy server on your home network. It does not host, sell or unlock any content. It plays free-to-air public streams, your own library and the sources you are entitled to.

What you get

  • Live TV: German TV (ARD, ZDF, arte, 3sat, DW and more where the broadcaster streams publicly), U.S. TV, local stations, news, kids, music, free streaming services, with now/next and a seven-day guide.
  • Sports Center: what's on now, NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, college, MLS, Bundesliga, Premier League, F1, UFC, golf and tennis schedules with live scores, and which of your channels carries each game.
  • Movies and series from your Galaxy library, with Continue Watching shared across Kodi, Android TV and Samsung.
  • Seven looks (Cinematic, Performance, Premium, Balanced, Minimal, Sports, Smart TV) using skins from the official Kodi repository.
  • English and German, following Kodi's language.
