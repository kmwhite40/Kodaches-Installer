Kodaches for Kodi

Free German and U.S. live TV, local Texas and Dallas - Fort Worth stations, a sports center with schedules and live scores, and thousands of classic films, in one Kodi add-on. No account, no subscription, no server.

Kodaches does not host, sell or unlock any content. It gathers free, publicly available streams listed by the open iptv-org project, public-domain films from the Internet Archive, and schedules from ESPN. Some broadcasters only stream inside their own country.

What you get

  • Live TV: German TV (ARD, ZDF, arte, 3sat, DW and more where the broadcaster streams publicly), U.S. TV, local stations, news, kids, music and free streaming services (Pluto TV, Samsung TV Plus, Tubi, Xumo and others).
  • Sports Center: sports channels plus NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, college, MLS, Bundesliga, Premier League, Champions League, F1, NASCAR, UFC, golf and tennis schedules with live scores and who broadcasts each game.
  • Movies: about 28,000 public-domain classic films, plus movie, series and documentary channels.
  • Streaming apps: YouTube, Disney+, Paramount+, ESPN, FOX Sports and PBS through their official add-ons (sign in with your own account).
  • Favorites and recently watched, automatic switching to a channel's backup stream, and a background clean-up that hides channels that stopped working.
  • The Kodaches build: its own skin and home screen, set up in one step.
  • Seven looks (Cinematic, Performance, Premium, Balanced, Minimal, Sports, Smart TV) using skins from the official Kodi repository.
  • English and German, following Kodi's language.
