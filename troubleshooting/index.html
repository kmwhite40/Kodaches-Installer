Help

"Could not load" when opening Kodaches

  • Kodaches needs internet access to load the channel lists. Check that other apps on the device are online.
  • If it worked before, Kodaches keeps using the last lists it loaded; try again later or choose Refresh channel lists.

"Couldn't connect to network server" when adding the source

  • Check the address letter by letter, including https:// and the final /kodi/.
  • Check the device's date and time; a wrong clock breaks secure connections.

A channel does not play

  • Free channels come from the broadcasters' public streams, which sometimes go offline. Kodaches switches to a channel's backup stream when there is one, and hides channels whose streams all failed in the last day (Add-on settings → Show channels that failed recently brings them back).
  • Many German public broadcasters (ARD, ZDF and their regional channels) only stream inside Germany. From other countries those channels fail; Kodaches does not get around this.
  • A channel that keeps buffering: choose the Kodaches (recommended) look, which uses a bigger video buffer. As a last resort, Add-on settings → Playback → Play HLS with InputStream Adaptive; it lowers quality instead of stalling, but crashed Kodi on some live streams in testing.

Local channels: where are ABC, CBS and PBS?

Only a few local stations publish free streams (in Dallas - Fort Worth: Fox 4, NBC 5 and UniMás). For the rest, use an antenna on your TV or the stations' own apps.

Kodi is slow on a Fire TV Stick

Use the Performance or Smart TV look, and restart the Fire TV once a week (Settings → My Fire TV → Restart).

Updates

Kodaches → Settings & looks → Check for updates. If Kodi says the repository cannot be reached, open manifest.json in a browser to check this site is up.
