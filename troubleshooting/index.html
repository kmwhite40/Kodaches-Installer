Help

"No Galaxy server was found"

  • The Kodi device must be on the same home network as Galaxy (not a guest Wi-Fi network).
  • Type the address yourself when asked: the one the Galaxy apps use, for example http://192.168.1.20:8080.
  • To change it later: Kodaches → Settings & looks → Galaxy server.

"Couldn't connect to network server" when adding the source

  • Check the address letter by letter, including https:// and the final /kodi/.
  • Check the device's date and time; a wrong clock breaks secure connections.

A channel does not play

  • Free channels come from the broadcasters' public streams, which sometimes go offline. Galaxy hides channels that stay offline; try another channel.
  • Many German public broadcasters (ARD, ZDF and their regional channels) only stream inside Germany. From other countries those channels fail; Kodaches does not get around this.
  • Stuttering on HLS channels: Kodaches → Settings & looks → Add-on settings → Playback → Play HLS with InputStream Adaptive (install InputStream Adaptive from the official Kodi repository first).

Local channels: where are ABC, CBS and PBS?

Only a few local stations publish free streams. For the rest, an antenna tuner (such as an HDHomeRun) connected to Galaxy adds them under My channels.

Kodi is slow on a Fire TV Stick

Use the Performance or Smart TV look, and restart the Fire TV once a week (Settings → My Fire TV → Restart).

Updates

Kodaches → Settings & looks → Check for updates. If Kodi says the repository cannot be reached, open manifest.json in a browser to check this site is up.
