Android TV and Google TV

NVIDIA Shield, Chromecast with Google TV, Google TV Streamer, and TVs with Android TV or Google TV (Sony, TCL, Hisense, Philips).

  1. Open the Google Play Store on the TV, search for Kodi (publisher: XBMC Foundation) and install it. This is the official build.
  2. Open Kodi once and allow it to access media if asked.
  3. Follow the five install steps.
If the Play Store says Kodi is not available for your device, download the official Android build from kodi.tv/download/android (ARMV8A 64-bit for most current devices) and install it with a file manager.

Good to know

  • Use the Performance or Smart TV look on devices with 2 GB of memory or less.
  • Add Kodaches to Kodi's home screen with a look (Kodaches → Settings & looks); it puts shortcuts such as Live TV and Sports Center in Favourites.
